# StyleNest FAQ

StyleNest is an online clothing shop. This document contains our official policies. Answer customers only from this document.

## Q1: How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days within Pakistan. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 working days in major cities (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad) for an extra fee of Rs. 300.

## Q2: How much does shipping cost?
Standard shipping costs Rs. 200. Shipping is free on orders above Rs. 3,000.

## Q3: Can I track my order?
Yes. After your order is dispatched, we send a tracking number by email and SMS. You can use it on our courier partner's website.

## Q4: What is your return policy?
You can return unused items with original tags within 7 days of delivery. The item must be in its original packaging. Sale items can only be exchanged, not returned.

## Q5: How do I start a return or exchange?
Email support@stylenest.example with your order number and the reason for return. We reply within 24 hours with pickup instructions.

## Q6: When will I get my refund?
Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 working days after we receive the returned item. The money goes back to your original payment method.

## Q7: What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Cash on Delivery, debit and credit cards, JazzCash and Easypaisa.

## Q8: My payment failed but money was deducted. What should I do?
Do not pay again. The amount is usually returned automatically within 3 to 5 working days. If it is not returned, email support@stylenest.example with your order number and a screenshot of the payment. Our team will resolve it within 48 hours.

## Q9: Can I cancel my order?
You can cancel your order within 12 hours of placing it, or before it is dispatched, whichever comes first. After dispatch, you must wait for delivery and use the return process.

## Q10: Can I change my delivery address?
Yes, if the order has not been dispatched yet. Email support@stylenest.example with your order number and the new address as soon as possible.

## Q11: What if I received a damaged or wrong item?
Please email support@stylenest.example within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and clear photos of the item. We will send a replacement or give a full refund at no extra cost.

## Q12: How do I find my size?
Each product page has a size chart in centimetres. If you are between two sizes, we recommend choosing the larger size. You can also exchange the size once for free within 7 days.

## Q13: What are your customer support hours?
Our support team is available Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM (Pakistan time). Emails sent on Sunday are answered on Monday.
